
# Visual Assets & Exploratory Data Visualizations

This directory contains all exported chart visualizations, architecture diagrams, and dashboard assets used throughout the repository documentation and executive reports.

---

## 1. Asset Inventory

| File Name | Description | Key Insight Highlighted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `monthly_revenue_proxy_trend.png` | Historical monthly revenue proxy & volume trend | Identifies Q4 holiday spikes (Nov/Dec) and post-holiday demand shifts. |
| `rating_distribution.png` | Overall distribution of customer review ratings (1–5 Stars) | Illustrates positive sentiment skew with a majority of 4 and 5-star ratings. |
| `top_10_product_categories.png` | Top 10 catalog categories ranked by sales & volume | Highlights leading revenue drivers across Skincare, Makeup, and Fragrance. |
| `recommendation_status_breakdown.png` | Proportion of customer product recommendations | Quantifies verified product recommendation rates across review samples. |
