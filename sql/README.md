# Sephora Skincare Catalog & Customer Review Insights

An end-to-end analytical breakdown of Sephora's skincare catalog and customer sentiment, evaluating **2,351 SKUs** across **1.09M reviews** to identify revenue drivers, inventory risks, and catalog optimization opportunities.

---

## Executive Summary

* **Total Sales Proxy Analyzed:** $192.5M
* **Total Reviews:** 1.09M
* **Total SKUs Analyzed:** 2,351
* **Catalog Average Rating:** 4.30 / 5.00
* **At-Risk Products:** 44 SKUs (High review volume, satisfaction < 3.50★)
* **Uncaptured Demand (OOS):** $8.13M across 124 out-of-stock items (avg. 4.25★)

## Core Findings

### 1. Revenue Concentration & Category Health
* **Treatments & Moisturizers:** Form the foundation of catalog revenue, with **Treatments** holding the highest loyalty (**77.2% recommendation rate**).
* **Tools & Devices:** Generate **$14.0M from only 76 SKUs**, driven by high average price points.
* **Lagging Categories:** **Lip Balms (60.1%)** and **Cleansers (66.3%)** show below-average recommendation rates.

### 2. High-Risk SKU Clusters
* **44 high-volume SKUs** maintain satisfaction scores below **3.50★**, creating return and churn risks.
* **Mineral Sunscreens:** Drunk Elephant, COOLA, and Supergoop! show recurring negative sentiment around white cast and heavy textures.
* **In-House Vulnerabilities:** Sephora Collection Clean Lip Balm & Scrub (**2.28★**) and Cleansing Wipes (**2.56★**) significantly underperform baseline customer satisfaction.

### 3. Catalog & Pricing Dynamics
* **Limited Editions:** Show strong pricing elasticity ($78.72 avg.) while retaining a **4.32★** average satisfaction score.
* **Online-Only Channels:** Deliver the highest customer rating (**4.33★**), proving direct-to-consumer digital merchandising viability.
* **Discount Inefficiencies:** On-sale items contribute only **0.3%** of total revenue and score lower (**4.15★**), signaling that deep discounting fails to drive sustainable volume.
