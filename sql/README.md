# Sephora Skincare Catalog & Customer Review Insights

## Quick Snapshot
* **Total Sales Proxy Analyzed:** $192.5M
* **Total Reviews:** 1.09M
* **Total Products:** 2,351 SKUs
* **Average Rating:** 4.30 / 5.00
* **Flagged Risk Products:** 44 SKUs (High volume, low satisfaction)

---

## 1. What the Data Tells Us

### 1. Two categories drive most of the business
* **Moisturizers ($60.6M)** and **Treatments ($48.4M)** generate over **56% of total revenue**.
* **High Tech Tools** brings in **$14.0M** from just 76 products due to premium pricing.
* **Treatments** has the most loyal buyers (**77.2% recommendation rate**), while **Lip Balms (60.1%)** and **Cleansers (66.3%)** have lower repeat endorsement.

### 2. A handful of brands dominate revenue
* The **top 10 brands generate 41% ($78.7M)** of all revenue.
* **La Mer** leads revenue ($13.4M) from high price points, while **Drunk Elephant ($11.6M)** and **Tatcha ($10.1M)** grow through mass volume (over 42k reviews each).
* Emerging brands like **DAMDAM (4.74★)** and **Dr. Lara Devgan (4.72★)** lead customer satisfaction, whereas **Topicals (3.66★)** and **NuFACE (3.88★)** receive mixed reviews.

### 3. 44 popular products are causing customer frustration
* **44 items** have over 50 reviews but rate below **3.50 stars**.
* Mineral sunscreens from major brands (Drunk Elephant, COOLA, Supergoop!) show frequent complaints around white cast and heavy texture.
* In-house items like **SEPHORA COLLECTION Clean Lip Balm & Scrub (2.28★)** and **Cleansing Wipes (2.56★)** are among the lowest-rated products in the catalog.

### 4. Exclusives and Limited Editions deliver strong value
* **Limited Edition items** carry a **62% higher price** ($78.72 vs $48.50) while holding a **4.32★** average rating.
* **Sephora Exclusives** bring in **$54.3M (28.2% of revenue)** at accessible price points ($40.35).
* **Online-Only products** earn the highest average satisfaction (**4.33★**).
* **Q1 (Jan–Mar)** sees the highest review activity (329k reviews), driven by post-holiday routine resets and gift usage.

---

## 2. Business Impact

* **Return & Churn Risk:** The 44 underperforming products create higher customer returns and increase customer support contacts.
* **Supplier Dependency:** Relying on a few legacy brands exposes the catalog to vendor supply shifts.
* **Missed Margin:** High customer willingness to buy limited-edition sets indicates room for more seasonal bundles.
* **Lost Demand:** **124 out-of-stock products** account for **$8.13M** in potential sales across well-rated items (4.25★).

---

## 3. Actionable Recommendations

### For Merchandising & Brands
* **Audit the 44 Flagged SKUs:** Review product descriptions and work with brand partners on application guidance for items with low recommendation rates (<30%).
* **Reformulate Low-Rated House Brand SKUs:** Update formulas for SEPHORA COLLECTION wipes and balms that score below 3.00★ to protect brand trust.
* **Highlight High-Satisfaction Indie Brands:** Feature top-rated lines like **DAMDAM** and **FaceGym** in discovery sections to diversify brand sales.

### For Pricing & Promotions
* **Expand Limited-Edition Gift Sets:** Release more seasonal, high-value bundles priced between $50 and $100, where customer satisfaction and conversion are strongest.
* **Limit Deep Discounting:** On-sale items make up only 0.3% of revenue and average lower ratings (4.15★). Focus on value-add bundles instead of steep clearance markdowns.

### For Inventory & Marketing
* **Restock High-Demand Favorites:** Prioritize inventory replenishment for the 124 out-of-stock everyday essentials to recover uncaptured demand.
* **Capitalize on the Q1 Engagement Peak:** Run targeted review prompts, loyalty points events, and replenishment reminders throughout January and February.
